## @imgly/background-removal

NEXORA REMOVE BG loads `@imgly/background-removal` version 1.7.0 as an in-browser background-removal engine.

- Project and corresponding source: https://github.com/imgly/background-removal-js
- License: GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)
- Package: https://www.npmjs.com/package/@imgly/background-removal
- Runtime/model assets are fetched by the browser from IMG.LY infrastructure on first use.

The NEXORA integration source is published in this repository in `remove-bg.html`, `remove-bg.css`, and `remove-bg.js`. The AI engine processes the selected image locally in the browser; the image itself is not sent to NEXORA.
